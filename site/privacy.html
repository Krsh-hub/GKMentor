Privacy policy

What we collect

Your email, a name or nickname, an age band (not your exact age), the exams you choose, and what you do on the site (saved notes, test results). If you sign in with Google we receive your Google name and verified email, never your Google password.

Why

To let you log in, keep your saved notes and test history across devices, and show content for your exams. We use it for nothing else without asking you first.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not show targeted ads based on your activity.

Under 18

If you choose the under-18 age band we keep only the data above and do not track you for advertising. A parent or guardian can contact us to review or delete the account.

Your choices

You can download your data or delete your account at any time from the Account menu. Deleting removes your account and saved data.

Passwords and security

Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. Sessions are kept in a secure cookie.
